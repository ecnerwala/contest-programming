Software package list

As Section E of the Contest Rules state, you may use only Google-provided computer equipment during the finals. You may choose to use your own keyboard and other materials permitted by Google. Below is the software package that will be installed on each machine used for both the Code Jam and the Distributed Code Jam Finals. 

We are supporting Linux-only at this year's Finals. This change allows for consistency with the compilers used on the competition platform. The below software list, we hope, will provide the best experience for contestants. We appreciate your understanding that we're unable to make any guarantees in fulfilling your requests. See the During the Contest section for more information on installing your own libraries and software as well.

 Questions? Contact codejam@google.com

During the contest

Please keep the below in mind:

  • The contest will last 4 hours.
  • We will provide pens, pencils, graph paper and notebooks.
  • You may bring up to 4 books, binders and notebooks.
  • You may only use your designated Google-provided machine during the Code Jam and Distributed Code Jam Finals. Using additional devices may result in your disqualification from the contest.
  • You will have a workstation of your own, which will run Debian.
  • You will have 30 minutes (or more) before the contest to prepare your workstation, with full Internet access. During the contest you will have access only to the Code Jam website and platform.
  • Bringing your own USB Sticks is okay, and is the best way of bringing code libraries and software. External hard drives are also fine; although we can't promise extra electrical sockets, we'll do our best to accommodate you.
  • Electronic music players and wireless communication devices may NOT be used during the contest. If you want to listen to music, please download it from your preferred music streaming player (Google Play, Pandora, Spotify, etc.) onto your computer prior to the start of the competition; we will wipe it for you when you're done. Headphones with bluetooth capabilities can only be plugged into your computer with bluetooth disabled.
  • You may install free or paid-for software on your machine so long as you (i) have all necessary rights to download, install, and use such software on the machine you use during the Code Jam and Distributed Code Jam Finals and (ii) your use of such software during the Finals solely pertains to your participation in the final round. If you are unsure whether you have the necessary rights to use free or paid-for software, you should not download, install, or use it on your machine at the Finals.
  • While we will try to prevent this from happening, it's possible that something will go wrong with your workstation. If that happens, we might have to switch you to a different workstation. This could result in losing part of your 30-minute internet access, or losing time during the contest. If this happens during the contest, you will NOT get Internet access; because of this we STRONGLY RECOMMEND bringing your extra software (compilers, interpreters, installers, binaries, code libraries, etc.) on physical media. Hopefully this will also help you make optimal use of your 30 minutes.
  • If you have 2-factor authentication set up for your Google account in a way that requires an electronic device (such as your phone) to log in, please remember that if you are logged out during the contest (e.g. if your machine crashes and needs a reboot), you won't be allowed to use the phone to log back in. Make sure to either deactivate the 2-factor for the duration of the contest, or have an alternate way to log in (like a written down single-use code) that doesn't require you using external electronics. It is your responsibility to make sure you can log into your account if needed. Your mobile device should be turned off during the competition. If you are seen using your mobile device or it rings during the competition, you are subject to disqualification.

Desktop Recording

  • We'll be recording your desktop during the competition to post on YouTube after the contest. People want to see what you're up to and learn from you!
  • In order to ensure your screen is recorded, please do not change screen resolution.
  • If you reboot, you will end the recording. Please alert our recording tech manager that you've rebooted. They'll be scanning the room throughout the competition checking the recordings as well.
