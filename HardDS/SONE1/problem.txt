The first line is N and M, indicating that there are N points and M queries in this tree
Then there are N-1 rows, each row x, y indicates that xy has an edge
Next is N rows, each row is a number, indicating the weight of each point
The next line indicates the root
Next is line M
The first number is K
K = 0 means the subtree is modified, followed by x, y, means the point weight of the subtree rooted at x is changed to y
K = 1 means changing roots, followed by x, means changing the root of this tree to x
K = 2 means chain modification, followed by x, y, z, means changing the point weight on the path of xy in this tree to z
K = 3 means that the subtree asks for min, followed by x, means the weight min of the midpoint of the subtree rooted at x
K = 4 means that the subtree asks for max, followed by x, means the weight of the midpoint of the subtree rooted at x
K = 5 means subtree addition, followed by x, y, means the weight of the midpoint of the subtree where x is the root + y
K = 6 means chain addition, followed by x, y, z, means changing the point weight on the path of xy in this tree to + z
K = 7 means the chain asks for min, followed by x, y, means the min on the path of xy in this tree
K = 8 means the chain asks for max, followed by x, y, means the max of points on the path of xy in this tree
K = 9 means changing the father, followed by x, y, means changing the father of x to y, if y does not operate in the x subtree.
K = 10 means that the chain asks for sum, followed by x, y, z, means that the sum of points on the path of xy in this tree
K = 11 means the subtree asks for sum, followed by x, it means the point weight sum of the subtree rooted at x
